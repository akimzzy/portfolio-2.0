# Writing

Mode: Read. This is a structural extension of the established portfolio; preserve its current visual system. The owner will revisit visual individuality later.

THESIS: A clear place to read Akeem's stories about building products, encountering problems, and learning from the work.

OWN-WORLD: Inherit the portfolio's Cabinet Grotesk typography and wordmark, 640px reading column, warm light/dark palette, blue links, 48px major section breaks, compact internal spacing, and restrained page chrome. Use Tailwind utilities throughout.

STORY: Find Writing in the main navigation, scan real posts newest first, read an individual story, and return to Writing or the portfolio's projects.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The shared header, a small Writing heading, a short description, and the post list. Before real content exists, show an honest empty state with a link to projects. Never invent blog posts or publication metrics.

FORM: Plain linked rows and dedicated article pages. Article content supports paragraphs, headings, lists, code blocks, and images. Long code scrolls inside its own block. Header links wrap at narrow widths, and the theme control follows the established behavior.

QUALITY BAR: Navigation must work in both directions, published posts must be ordered newest first, drafts must be unavailable by direct URL, article text must fit mobile, and the homepage's projects and experience must retain their existing interaction and spacing.

No approved raster comp or new design world is involved. Additional features require the owner's approval.
